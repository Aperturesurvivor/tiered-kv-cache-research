## Descriptive resource comparison

[`derived-costs.json`](derived-costs.json) retains every fixture's paired request and work counts plus mean, median and range by length and arm; it is reproducible with `python derived-costs.py` from this directory. At 8K, mean request time was 5.72 s stock and 77.41 s CPU reranker; at 32K, 19.56 s stock and 96.26 s reranker. Mean emitted tokens were respectively 134.2/136.5 at 8K and 131.8/128.8 at 32K. These are single observations with potentially different output and forward work, not a matched-token timing test. The separate matched-workload cost experiment addresses that question.

The sidecar separately reports mount, initialization, checkpoint, shared prefill, GPU allocated/reserved peaks and sampled host RSS. Its accounted-service sum is mount + initialization + request + checkpoint; it excludes shared prefill and cleanup; reranker index construction is included in initialization and is not separately timed. Request includes durable round-file serialization. Logical archive reads and OS process read counters are reported by their actual scopes and are not physical SSD traffic; allocator peaks and sampled RSS do not establish bounded whole-process memory.
